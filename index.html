Hi, I'm Fernanda! A Miami-based UGC creator originally from Brazil. I moved to the U.S. in 2022 with my husband and our two Pomeranians (Biscuit and Pompom 🐾).

Since our marriage, we've built a lifestyle centered around health, consistency, and growing together, so most of our days revolve around fitness, wellness routines, and creating a balanced, intentional life. We also love to travel (from Disney to NYC!) and always make time to explore new places.

Before becoming a creator, I spent nearly a decade working as a lawyer in Brazil, along with experience in therapy, customer experience, and business development, which shaped my strategic, people-centered, and persuasive approach to content.

Today, I blend UX research, consumer psychology, and real-life storytelling to create content that feels authentic and drives results.
